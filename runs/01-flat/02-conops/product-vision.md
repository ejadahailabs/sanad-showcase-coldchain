# Product vision — MRTM

> **MANUAL** (F-12). DRAFT.

**For** clinics that store vaccines and medicines in fridges,
**who** cannot watch the temperature all day and night,
**the** Medical Refrigerator Temperature Monitor
**is** a small stand-alone device
**that** alerts staff within about a minute of a real temperature problem and keeps a history nobody can edit.
**Unlike** a thermometer read by hand, it never sleeps and never forgets.

Think of it as a smoke alarm with a diary, for the medicine fridge.
