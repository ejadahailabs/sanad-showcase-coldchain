# Buzzer on excursion

## Description

The monitor shall sound the buzzer within 5 s of excursion confirmation.

## Rationale

The buzzer is the alert that reaches a nurse out of sight of the device.

## Verification

Test: measure the time from confirmation to buzzer onset.
