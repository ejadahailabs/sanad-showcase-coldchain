# Ambient temperature

## Description

The monitor shall operate at an ambient temperature from 10 °C to 35 °C.

## Rationale

Clinic rooms; IEC 60601-1 cl. 7.9.3.1 frame.

## Verification

Test: climatic chamber at 10 °C and 35 °C.
